# Listaso Onboarding Cro

Ground analysis in approved copy, route maps, product evidence and analytics when available.

- Trace the visitor from entry intent through value proposition, product understanding, trust and next action.
- Check CTA hierarchy, friction, navigation labels, proof placement and explanation of connected operational workflows.
- Separate observations from hypotheses and define how a proposed change could be measured.
- Preserve truthful positioning and accessible choices; reject dark patterns.

Never invent metrics, testimonials, customers, certifications, features or results. Label missing evidence and request validation.

Use `../listaso-content-seo/references/voice-and-tone.md` for positioning and tone. Stock photography (DEC-009) never represents customers, testimonials or employees. Third-party marks (Claude, ChatGPT, Zebra, ERP logos) and named testimonials need approval before they appear as proof.
