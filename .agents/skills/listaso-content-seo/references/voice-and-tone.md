# Listaso voice and tone

Decision: DEC-007. Evidence: `docs/BRAND_ANALYSIS.md`.

## Positioning (CONFIRMED by Erick)

Listaso competes in a crowded B2B distribution-software market. It presents
itself as a partner that stays ahead of what current and new customers need.

From the brand guide (CONFIRMED):
- Values: professionalism, clarity, confidence, innovation.
- Concept: Flexibility + Brand recognition + Growth.
- Mission: simplify operational complexity across the customer's commercial
  chain.
- Vision: the reference for B2B commercial and logistics technology in Latin
  America and North America.

## Tone

Professional, warm and friendly.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Speak to distributors, wholesalers and manufacturers about their daily operation (routes, orders, inventory, collections) | Generic SaaS hype ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "next-gen") |
| Use short sentences and active voice; the customer is the subject | Jargon without explanation, or acronyms on first use |
| Be warm and specific: "your reps", "your warehouse", "your customers" | Cold corporate distance or excessive exclamation marks |
| Make confident claims backed by approved evidence | Invented metrics, customers, certifications, prices or integrations |
| Show vanguard through concrete capability (SAMI, integrations, mobile) | Fear-based or competitor-bashing copy |

## Copy conventions

- **Headings** may use the brand device: an opening word or short phrase in bold
  italic (see the `listaso-design-system` brand foundations). The phrase must
  read naturally without the styling.
- **Primary CTA:** "Book a demo", as in the brand guide and the legacy nav. In
  Spanish, use the approved equivalent once it exists. Keep one primary CTA
  label per page.
- **Taglines.** The example lines in the brand guide (p33) and "Powered by People"
  are not approved taglines. Do not publish them as slogans without Marketing
  approval.
- **SAMI** copy can be more conversational and future-facing, but it follows the
  same honesty rules.
- **Excluded.** Prices and credits seen in Guide Sami (for example "SAMI Credit
  $9.99") are not approved content.
- **Spanish** content is reviewed, never machine-translated at runtime. The form
  of address (tú or usted) is UNKNOWN; ask Marketing before writing Spanish
  copy.
- **Testimonials and customer names** from any source need written approval
  before use.
