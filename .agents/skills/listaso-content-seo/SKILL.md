# Listaso Content Seo

Read the route entry, approved source copy and `docs/PROJECT_MASTER.md`.

- Preserve terminology and explain operational flows concretely.
- Maintain a logical H1, heading hierarchy, unique title/description, internal links, Open Graph and relevant structured content.
- Keep English and Spanish reviewable and aligned; no runtime machine translation for official content.
- Write useful alt text and leave decorative images empty.
- Route unsupported statements to the content owner.

Never invent features, prices, metrics, certifications, testimonials, customers, availability or commercial terms. Do not publish incomplete translations.

Load `references/voice-and-tone.md` before writing or reviewing copy. The tone is professional, warm and friendly (DEC-007). The brand guide's sample lines are not approved taglines.
