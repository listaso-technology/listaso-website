## English proposed text

### 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Listaso Inc. («Listaso», «we», «us») provides a public website and, under customer agreements, software and related services for business operations. This Policy describes personal information we handle through our public website, sales and support interactions, and the Listaso services and mobile applications, to the extent the relevant feature is enabled. It does not make every described feature available to every customer.

**Controller contact:** [confirm legal entity, postal address and privacy email]. The applicable customer agreement and any data processing addendum («DPA») govern customer data processed to provide the services. A customer may control the purposes and means of processing information about its personnel, buyers, recipients and other contacts; in that context Listaso processes information on the customer's instructions, subject to the contract and applicable law. Listaso may determine its own purposes for account administration, billing, security, website operation and its direct business relationships. Counsel must confirm these roles for each actual data flow.

### 2. Information we may process

The categories below apply only where collected or enabled and may include information supplied by the person, the customer, an authorized integration, a device or a service provider:

| Context | Potential categories |
| --- | --- |
| Website and communications | Name, company, business contact details, message content, demo preferences, support inquiries, form submissions, consent choices, IP address, browser and device information, pages visited and interaction data. |
| Customer accounts and personnel | Account identifiers, credentials or authentication metadata, assigned roles, support history, activity logs and subscription or billing contacts. |
| Customer, supplier and financial workflows | Customer and supplier contacts, quotes, individual prices or discounts, orders, credits, invoices, bills, receivables, payment status or references, surveys, customer photographs, notes and documents. Whether any payment card data is handled must be verified separately. |
| Warehouse, delivery and production | Product and inventory records, warehouse locations, stock movements, picks, returns, truck and delivery assignments, routes, item identifiers, forecasts and bills of material. Some records may identify assigned workers or delivery recipients. |
| Personnel, mobile and field functions | Employee records, roles, timesheets, tasks, commissions and authentication records; device and app diagnostics; where enabled and permitted, GPS or route location, photographs, signatures, scans or other field activity. The collection interval, visibility and controls must be confirmed per app and module. |
| Imports and commerce | Uploaded customer, vendor, product, order, image and custom-field files; buyer accounts and ordering information; synchronization records with connected systems. Temporary files, logs and error records require separate confirmation. |

| SAMI or other AI features | Prompts, connected business information used to answer them, files or context supplied by the user, outputs, feedback and technical logs, to the extent such features are enabled. Provider, retention and model training practices must be confirmed before publication. |

We ask customers not to provide information that a feature does not need. A customer's configuration and instructions determine which business data its users submit or connect. A customer is responsible for ensuring it has the rights, notices and permissions required to supply that data and use the selected features, including appropriate notices to its personnel and business contacts, subject to the allocation of responsibilities in the applicable agreement. A feature's availability does not establish that all associated data is collected in every customer account.

### 3. Why we use information

Depending on the context, Listaso uses information to respond to inquiries and schedule demonstrations; evaluate and establish a business relationship; provide, configure, secure and support the contracted services; administer orders and subscriptions; synchronize information through integrations selected by the customer; operate field and reporting functions; investigate errors and misuse; comply with law; and send business communications where permitted. Any use of Customer Data to improve services beyond the contracted purpose requires a documented authorization and legal review. AI features, if enabled, may use submitted context to generate requested responses. The precise purposes for a custom function are recorded in its approved scope. We will not treat a new purpose or category as automatically covered merely because the platform supports custom work.

Where a law requires a stated legal basis, the applicable basis must be identified for each purpose and jurisdiction before publication. Consent for optional marketing or tracking, where required, is separate from acceptance of a service contract. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that has another valid basis or that occurred before withdrawal, subject to applicable law.

### 4. Website technologies and third parties

The public site may use essential technologies for operation and, when configured, analytics or similar tools. WordPress content, Zoho forms or chat, Calendly scheduling, Google Analytics or Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity and other third-party content must be disclosed according to what actually loads on each site and under each consent choice. An embedded form or external link may send information directly to its provider; the provider may also process technical information under its own notices. The current provider and cookie schedule, purposes, lifetimes and control mechanism must be supplied before publication. A separate cookie notice may provide these details.

The customer may authorize integrations with accounting, inventory, commerce or other systems. The data exchanged, direction of transfer and permissions depend on the integration and customer configuration. Listaso's marketing references to a system do not mean it is connected for a particular customer. We share information with hosting, communications, support, security, analytics, AI or other service providers only to the extent needed for an authorized purpose and subject to applicable contractual protections. A current list of relevant sub-processors and their functions should be maintained where required by contract or law. We may also disclose information when legally required or in a corporate transaction, subject to applicable safeguards and notices.

### 5. International transfers, security and retention

Information may be processed in locations where Listaso or its authorized providers operate. The actual processing locations and transfer safeguards must be listed and assessed for the applicable markets. We use administrative, technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information and services, as specified in the customer agreement and security documentation. No system can be guaranteed free of risk. Security commitments, incident notices and access controls must match verified practice.

We retain information for the period needed for the relevant purpose and contractual or legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it according to a documented schedule. Customer data return and deletion, backups, support records, analytics, prospect records and AI logs may follow different schedules. Those schedules and any exceptions must be confirmed before publication. The 2022 statement that comments are retained indefinitely must not be carried forward unless the feature and period are verified.

### 6. Choices, rights and requests

Depending on applicable law and our role, a person may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection, withdraw consent, or exercise other rights. A customer controls many records submitted through the service; a person whose information is held on a customer's behalf should normally contact that customer, and Listaso will assist as required by the contract and law. Requests about information Listaso controls may be sent to [confirm privacy email]. We may need to verify identity and may retain information where law or a valid obligation requires it. The applicable time limits, appeal process and jurisdiction-specific disclosures must be added after Legal determines the markets covered.

### 7. Children, changes and contact

The public website and business services are directed to organizations and their authorized personnel, not to children. If Listaso learns it has collected children's information outside an authorized customer use, it will evaluate and address the matter under applicable law. This Policy may change when practices or requirements change. Material changes will be communicated in a manner appropriate to the affected relationship and law. **Effective date:** [confirm]. **Privacy contact and postal address:** [confirm].

### 3. Para qué usamos los datos

Según el contexto, Listaso usa información para responder consultas y agendar demostraciones; evaluar y establecer una relación comercial; prestar, configurar, proteger y apoyar los servicios contratados; administrar pedidos y suscripciones; sincronizar información mediante integraciones elegidas por el cliente; operar funciones de campo y reportes; investigar errores y usos indebidos; cumplir la ley; y enviar comunicaciones comerciales cuando se permita. Cualquier uso de Datos del Cliente para mejorar servicios más allá de la finalidad contratada requiere autorización documentada y revisión jurídica. Las funciones de IA habilitadas pueden usar el contexto enviado para generar respuestas solicitadas. Las finalidades precisas de una función a medida se registran en su alcance aprobado. Una nueva finalidad o categoría no queda cubierta automáticamente sólo porque la plataforma permita desarrollos a medida.

Cuando una ley exige indicar una base jurídica, se debe identificar la aplicable para cada finalidad y jurisdicción antes de publicar. El consentimiento para marketing o seguimiento opcional, cuando se requiera, es independiente de la aceptación de un contrato de servicio. Retirar el consentimiento no afecta el tratamiento que tenga otra base válida o que se realizó antes del retiro, sujeto a la ley aplicable.

### 4. Tecnologías del sitio y terceros

El sitio público puede usar tecnologías esenciales para funcionar y, cuando estén configuradas, herramientas de analítica o similares. El contenido de WordPress, los formularios o chat de Zoho, la agenda de Calendly, Google Analytics o Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity y otros contenidos de terceros deben informarse conforme a lo que realmente carga cada sitio y cada preferencia de consentimiento. Un formulario incrustado o enlace externo puede enviar información directamente a su proveedor; éste también puede tratar datos técnicos según sus propios avisos. Antes de publicar se debe aportar el inventario vigente de proveedores y cookies, sus finalidades, duraciones y mecanismos de control. Una política de cookies separada puede detallar estos puntos.

El cliente puede autorizar integraciones con sistemas contables, de inventario, comercio u otros. Los datos intercambiados, la dirección de transferencia y los permisos dependen de la integración y de la configuración del cliente. Una referencia comercial a un sistema no implica que esté conectado para cada cliente. Compartimos información con proveedores de alojamiento, comunicaciones, soporte, seguridad, analítica, IA u otros servicios sólo en la medida necesaria para una finalidad autorizada y bajo las protecciones contractuales aplicables. Debe mantenerse una lista vigente de subprocesadores relevantes cuando lo exija el contrato o la ley. También podemos divulgar información por exigencia legal o en una transacción corporativa, sujetándonos a las salvaguardas y avisos aplicables.

### 5. Transferencias internacionales, seguridad y conservación

La información puede tratarse en lugares donde operen Listaso o sus proveedores autorizados. Las ubicaciones reales y salvaguardas de transferencia deben inventariarse y evaluarse para los mercados aplicables. Utilizamos medidas administrativas, técnicas y organizativas apropiadas para la información y el servicio, según el acuerdo con el cliente y la documentación de seguridad. Ningún sistema puede garantizar ausencia total de riesgo. Los compromisos de seguridad, aviso de incidentes y control de acceso deben coincidir con la práctica verificada.

Conservamos la información durante el periodo necesario para su finalidad y las obligaciones contractuales o legales; después la eliminamos o desidentificamos conforme a un calendario documentado. La devolución y eliminación de datos del cliente, respaldos, registros de soporte, analítica, prospectos y registros de IA pueden tener plazos distintos. Esos plazos y sus excepciones deben confirmarse antes de publicar. La afirmación de 2022 sobre conservar comentarios indefinidamente no debe trasladarse sin verificar la función y el periodo.
